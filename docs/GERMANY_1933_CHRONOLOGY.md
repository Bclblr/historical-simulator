# Germany 1933 Chronology

Milestone 66 establishes the first chronological content foundation for Germany in 1933.

The initial chronology contains ten dated historical events covering the appointment of Adolf Hitler as Chancellor, the Reichstag fire and emergency decree, the March election, establishment of Dachau, the Enabling Act, the anti-Jewish boycott, the Civil Service Law, book burnings and the establishment of the one-party state.

The chronology stores concise factual summaries only. It does not reproduce propaganda, glorify the regime or treat later player decisions as historical fact. Playable event-card design begins separately at milestone 68.
