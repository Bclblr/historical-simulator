# Germany 1933 — First Institution and 20-Event Content Set

Milestones 67–69 establish the first institution selection and expand the historical content foundation to twenty events.

The first selectable institution is the Reich Government, modeled as the national executive institution. Party organizations, police/security bodies, state governments and other institutions remain separate entities for later expansion.

The twenty-event chronology is factual source content, not a set of endorsements or rewards. It documents the rapid dismantling of democratic institutions, political repression, antisemitic exclusion, forced sterilization policy, control of unions, press and culture, and foreign-policy developments during 1933.

These HistoricalEvent records provide the event/card content pool. Decision branches and counterfactual consequences remain explicitly separate from the historical record.
