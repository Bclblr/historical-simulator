# Germany 1933 — 50-Event Chronology

Milestones 70–72 expand the 1933 Germany chronology from twenty to fifty dated historical records.

The expanded set covers the political crisis preceding the January government, consolidation of executive power, destruction of the multi-party system, federal centralization, discriminatory legislation, suppression of independent unions, control of education, culture, press and broadcasting, changes in policing, and selected foreign-policy developments.

The chronology is descriptive historical data. It does not reproduce ideological propaganda as player instruction, reward persecution, or classify counterfactual player actions as historical facts.

Research basis for this expansion includes the United States Holocaust Memorial Museum Holocaust Encyclopedia and the Deutsches Historisches Museum LeMO 1933 chronology. Individual source/citation records will receive the dedicated academic source audit at milestone 74.
